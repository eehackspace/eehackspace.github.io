## How to join No appointment needed - just turn up on a Tuesday evening and say hello. 1. **Visit first.** Come along on a Tuesday evening - socials from 7:30pm, tours and inductions from around 8pm. It's worth checking on [Discord](https://discord.gg/KftFA5S) before travelling any distance, in case plans change at short notice. 2. **Get your induction.** Inductions are generally held on Tuesdays at 8pm and cover how the space works and how to use it safely. Some individual machines have their own inductions too - see the [induction pages on the wiki](https://wiki.eehack.space/index.php/Induction). 3. **Sign up on the member portal.** Sign up [here](https://checkout.eehack.space). A trustee has to enable your account. 4. **Set up your monthly subscription.** After you've joined and been inducted, you can access the space with a £5 monthly subscription. We also suggest a £15 donation, which is eligible for Gift Aid, although you are welcome to donate more or less if you prefer. Your subscription can be cancelled or restarted at any time without needing to repay the original £5 membership fee. 5. **Collect your access fob.** Pick it up from a trustee on a Tuesday evening, and you'll have 24/7 access to the space (with some restrictions on noisy tools late in the day, out of respect for our neighbours). {: .steps}
