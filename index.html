What is a Hackspace?

A lot of negative connotations surround the word "hack", but hacking is the repurposing and learning of things. A hackspace is a shared space for people who mess around with technology for fun. By creating a shared space to do this, we provide access to better facilities than we could each have at home, as well as opportunities to collaborate, learn, and socialise.

East Essex Hackspace

East Essex Hackspace has been setup in an old sports pavilion in Hawkwell. We have a metal & wood workshop, computer lab with electronics workbench, 3D printing lab, laser cutting room, Wazer waterjet cutter, wet lab for brewing & photography, and social/lecture space.

Workshops

Workshops are free! You don't need to be a member to learn with us - just check out our upcoming events and pop in at the advertised time. (There may be a small charge for materials used)

Join us!

Come along to one of our Tuesday evening open nights (from around 8pm) for a tour and induction, and to meet some of the members. Lifetime membership is a one-off £5, then access to the workshops is a £5pcm subscription - we also suggest a £15pcm donation (Gift Aid eligible) to help cover bills, consumables and equipment, though you're welcome to give more or less. You can join and subscribe at checkout.eehack.space.
