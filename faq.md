## My household gizmo is broken - can you fix it for me?

Quite possibly! We run a public **repair café** where volunteers help fix broken household items - electricals, clothing, toys and more. <!-- VERIFY: repair café frequency — the wiki and current site say "typically every three months, usually on a Sunday"; more recent reports suggest roughly monthly on the last Saturday. Check with the events team before publishing a frequency. --> Check the [event calendar](https://wiki.eehack.space/index.php/Main_Page#Calendar_of_Events) or our [Facebook events](https://www.facebook.com/groups/eastessexhackspace) for the next confirmed date. If you'd like to volunteer as a fixer, [get in touch](mailto:info@eehack.space).

## Can you help me make this exciting new thing?

We can - or rather, we can help you gain the skills and knowledge to make it yourself. Our members come from all sorts of backgrounds and have numerous skills between them. Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/KftFA5S) and someone can usually give you some advice.

## Can you make this thing for me?

Usually our answer has to be "Sorry, no we can't." We aren't a manufacturing service and we don't take commissions. Our aim, as a hackspace, is to provide a place where the community can make things for themselves and learn new skills along the way - so while we can't make it for you, we'd love to help you learn to make it yourself.

## I've never used a workshop before - is that a problem?

Not at all. Most tools just need a short induction before you use them, and members are happy to show you how. Come along on a Tuesday evening and tell us what you'd like to learn.

## Can I bring my children?

Membership is 18+ only and unaccompanied children are not allowed on site. But as a community space, families are welcome, as long as any children are supervised at all times.

## What are the rules?

We keep them short: a small set of [rules](https://wiki.eehack.space/index.php/Rules) and a [health and safety policy](https://wiki.eehack.space/index.php/Health_and_Safety_Policy) for using the facilities, and a [code of conduct](https://wiki.eehack.space/index.php/Code_of_Conduct) that applies on site and in our online spaces such as Discord. Problems are handled through our [grievance procedure](https://wiki.eehack.space/index.php/Grievance_Procedure). We collect some personal data as required by law and to provide access - see the [privacy policy](https://wiki.eehack.space/index.php/East_Essex_Hackspace:Privacy_policy).

## When can I visit?

Tuesday evenings are our open and social night - socials from 7:30pm, with tours and inductions for new members from around 8pm. No appointment needed. See [how to find us](/find-us/).
